# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Being kind and respectful to other people
- Accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the project
- Showing empathy toward other contributors

Behaviour that does not:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and will take fair
corrective action in response to behaviour they consider inappropriate. They
may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

Report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour by opening a
confidential report through GitHub, or by contacting a maintainer directly.
All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the
reporter's privacy and security will be respected.
